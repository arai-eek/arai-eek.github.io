Manifesto

The Arai-eek Zine Library is more than an archive; it is a mycelial network of knowledge spanning 20 years. To navigate this heterogeneous mass of 80+ PDF variants, we deployed an autonomous Agentic Curation Layer.

This is not simple digitization. This is the synthesis of human curatorial intent with machine-scale narrative processing.

◈ SPECULATIVE SYNTHESIS WARNING: While the extraction pipelines use factual text from the zines, the "Deep Info" dossiers and poetic commentary are co-authored by an AI agent. These represent narrative extrapolations and creative interpretations intended to fit the Cyber-Tropical aesthetic of the portal.

Process Architecture

01

Semantic Extraction

We bypassed traditional metadata by performing a "Deep Scan" of the PDF bodies. Using autonomous `pdftotext` extraction, we harvested fragments from the initial layers of each zine to identify core themes, languages, and authorship markers.

02

Narrative Synthesis

Extracted data was passed to the DeepSeek-V4 model family. Through high-fidelity reasoning, the agent synthesized "Speculative Research Dossiers"—poetic interpretations that connect the technical protocols of the zines to the broader Cyber-Tropical aesthetic of the Hackteria network.

03

Spore Taxonomy & Reconciliation

The archive suffered from 20 years of heterogeneous naming conventions. We implemented a self-correcting reconciliation pipeline that mapped AI-generated "slugs" back to physical filenames, ensuring a 100% synchronized database across the static catalogue and the interactive map.
