Arai-eek Lab - The Chiang Mai Chapter (April-May 2026)

Welcome to the Arai-eek Lab. “Arai-eek” means “what else?” in Thai — a simple question that opens infinite possibilities. Both programs are built on that question: What else can art become when it grows like an organism? What else can we create with living materials, waste, microbes, and time? What else is possible when technology is not only digital, but ecological, handmade, and shared?

Arai-eek Co-Laboratory & Arai-eek Lab Residency is a format of mutual learning and knowledge exchange rooted in a shared fascination with biology — from ecological science to synthetic biology — merged with digital technologies and traditional craft. We grow, ferment, decompose, hack, observe, and listen. From small experiments to shared meals, from silent observation to chaotic collaboration — this is a living lab where ideas evolve through people, place, and time.

Part of Technobiological Futures Co-Laboratories

The Arai-eek Lab is a key node in the Technobiological Futures Co-Laboratories programme (2026-2027), a multi-phase initiative spanning Thailand, Switzerland, Indonesia, and India. Built upon 15 years of deep collaboration, experiments, and Do-It-With-Others (DIWO) methodologies developed by the Global Hackteria Network and SGMK (Swiss Mechatronic Art Society), this programme catalyzes South-South exchange. Locally in Chiang Mai, this chapter is co-hosted and made possible by our main partners tomorrow.lab and Pa Rang Cafe & Art Stay. It is co-funded by “Synergies” via the Swiss Arts Council Pro Helvetia.

Lannafuturism Themes

What happens when centuries-old Lanna craft knowledge meets open-source technology, biological materials, and experimental art practice?

🧵

Craft

730 years of living knowledge. Weaving, ceramics, and woodwork merging with digital fabrication.

🔬

Science & Tech

Partnering with universities to explore DIY biology, nanoscience, and appropriate open-source tech.

🎨

Art & Community

Building on Chiang Mai's 30-year history of independent artist-run spaces and maker collectives.

🌱

Food & Farm

Rethinking agriculture, microbial fermentation, and the transformation of waste into bio-material.

✨ Stikka Factory

Check out our sticker art graphics in the design repository, or try printing remotely to our local label printer via the Stikka Factory Live!

The live link connects directly to our printit station (originally an experiment from CCC camp). You can send designs remotely to print dithered images, custom labels with QR codes, DIY PCB etching masks, AI-generated text-to-image art, and even cats—all printed on-site in Chiang Mai!
